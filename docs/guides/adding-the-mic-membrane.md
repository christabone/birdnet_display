# Adding the Waterproof Sticker to the Outdoor Microphone

The birdsong microphone has a tiny hole on the **bottom** of its box. It's covered with a bug mesh for now. This sticker makes it fully waterproof while still letting sound through. It takes about 5 minutes.

## What to order

On AliExpress (or similar), search for:

- **"IP67 acoustic vent sticker 6mm"**, or
- **"waterproof acoustic membrane mic"**

Choose a round or square sticker **6–10 mm across**. Get a pack; they're cheap, and spares are handy.

## Steps

1. **Pick a dry day.** Unplugging isn't required. The microphone is low voltage, and you'll only be touching the outside of the box.
2. **Lift off the rain hood.** It slides straight up off the box; no tools needed.
3. **Find the small hole** on the bottom face of the box, covered with fine mesh.
4. **Clean around the hole** with a little rubbing alcohol on a cotton pad or tissue. Let it dry for a minute. The sticker needs a clean, dry surface to seal.
5. **Peel the sticker** by its edge. Don't touch the sticky side or the shiny centre (that's the part sound goes through).
6. **Centre it over the hole** (right over the mesh is fine) and **press firmly around the edge** for about 10 seconds. Don't press hard on the centre.
7. **Slide the rain hood back on.**

That's it. The display should keep showing birds as normal. If birds seem quieter afterward, tell Chris; he can raise the microphone's sensitivity remotely.
